Dubai Market Explorer

One bubble per community. Switch the layout and the same bubbles glide into a new arrangement, each one answering a different question about Dubai's property market. Click a bubble to see that community's history underneath.

How to read this, and how the data was prepared
  • Bubbles: every bubble is one community. Area is the number of sales. Colour is the median price per m² in 7 classes with an equal number of communities each, so differences stay visible even though a few communities are very expensive. The colour is the same in every layout, so you can follow a community from one view to the next.
  • Layouts: the map uses the community's real position. Price vs size places each community by typical size (m²) and price per m². Market maturity groups communities by the share of sales that are off-plan (under a third, a third to two thirds, over two thirds). The two bar layouts rank the top 20 by number of sales or by median price per m².
  • Reliability: a median from a handful of sales is not trustworthy, so communities with fewer sales than the slider value are hidden (default 25). Lowering it shows more communities but with shakier medians.
  • Data used: sales of units and villas registered 1 Jan 2015 to 31 Aug 2026. Prices outside 2,000 to 80,000 AED/m² and sizes outside 15 to 2,000 m² are dropped as implausible. The file is a 10% sample, so counts are about a tenth of real volumes, but medians are not affected. Community names and positions come from Dubai Municipality's dubai.geojson, linked through comm_num.
  • Zoom and pan (Map layout): scroll the mouse wheel or pinch on a touchpad to zoom towards the pointer, drag to pan, or use the slider and the + and − buttons. The boundaries, the bubbles and their sizes are all driven by the same zoom parameter, so they always stay aligned. Bubbles grow a little more slowly than the map, so crowded bubbles separate as you zoom in, and more community names appear.
  • Animation: the animation is done inside Vega-Lite. Each bubble is a rectangle whose position, size and corner roundness are computed for every layout, and a parameter blends between two layouts over about a second, with a small delay per bubble. Fully rounded corners make a square look like a circle and a long thin rectangle look like a bar.
