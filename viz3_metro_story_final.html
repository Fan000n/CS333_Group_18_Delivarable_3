    Data notes: where the numbers come from
    • Venues: metro_venues_total.csv from the course repository: the number of Foursquare venues of each of 10 categories around each of the 56 metro stations (Red Line including the Route 2020 branch, Green Line and Expo). The data was collected around 2020, so newer areas are under-counted. Station names in this file are the 2020 names.
    • Map: station positions come from the same file. Lines join the stations in order, so they are schematic. Community boundaries are from dubai.geojson (Dubai Municipality), drawn only for context.
    • Sales (finale only): sales of units and villas registered 1 Jan 2015 to 31 Aug 2026 in transactions.csv, with prices of 2,000 to 80,000 AED/m² and sizes of 15 to 2,000 m² (implausible values removed). Each sale is linked to a station through the transactions' nearest_metro_en, which is assigned per area, not per street. That column uses station names from different years, so names were matched to station numbers by hand where they had changed (for example “Sharaf Dg” is the station now numbered R33). The script is prepare_sales.py. Tram and monorail stops are not metro stations and are excluded, and 38 of the 56 stations have at least one linked sale.
    • Sample: the transactions file is a random 10% sample, so real sales counts are about ten times higher. Comparisons between stations are still fair.
    • Correlation: computed on the 38 stations with sales: Pearson correlation of venues and sales, and Spearman rank correlation (which ignores the huge range in sales).
